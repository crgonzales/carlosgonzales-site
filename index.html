What I build

A full GNC stack, end to end.

The docking simulator is where the GNC work lives. Every piece below is implemented, tested, and running in the browser.

  • Dynamics RK4 Clohessy–Wiltshire truth dynamics, rigid-body attitude with thruster torques, and a 16-jet RCS model with injectable failure states — verified against a closed-form analytic oracle in CI.
  • Navigation ANEES-gated 6-state EKF for relative translation, plus an attitude MEKF with gyro-bias estimation and star-tracker updates.
  • Control V-bar guidance with PID and LQR, and a constrained active-set QP MPC terminal approach enforcing approach-corridor and capture-envelope limits.
  • Allocation & Safety Bounded-NNLS force/torque allocation across the jet set, with two-level corridor monitoring and automatic passive-abort safing burns.
  • Verification A seeded Monte Carlo harness scoring docking outcome, propellant use, and time margin across randomized dispersions.
